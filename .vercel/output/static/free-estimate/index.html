How We Work

What You Will Not Get From Us

No same day pressure

There is no discount that vanishes when we leave your driveway. That tactic exists to stop you comparing quotes, which is exactly what you should be doing on a purchase this size. Take a week. Take a month.

No selling you a roof you do not need

A meaningful share of the roofs we inspect have years of life left, and we say so in writing. If a repair will do, we will quote the repair rather than steering you toward a replacement.

No vague lump sums

Every estimate is itemized. If our number is higher than another quote, the line items will show you precisely why, usually in tear off, underlayment, or flashing work that the cheaper quote left out.

No subcontracted crews

The crew that quotes your roof is the crew that installs it. You are not handed off to a team you have never met after signing, which is how accountability gets lost on a job.
