Our Story

Over a Decade on New England Roofs

Vincenzo’s Roofing was built by Vincenzo, who has spent more than ten years working on New Hampshire roofs.

That experience was earned in the conditions this region actually produces: Seacoast homes taking salt air and wind straight off the water, inland colonials with ice damming every February, and flat commercial roofs where one blocked drain becomes somebody’s ruined stock room.

He built this company around the things he saw done badly elsewhere. Written estimates instead of lump sums. Our own crews instead of subcontracted labor. Warranties in writing, registered before we leave. Accountability sitting with one company rather than being passed down a chain until nobody owns the problem.

We are based in Seabrook, which puts us within a short drive of the whole Seacoast and on the road across the rest of New Hampshire daily. More than 100 completed projects later, Vincenzo still answers the emergency line himself.
